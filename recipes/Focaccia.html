Focaccia Recipe

Description

Making bread is not the easiest task out there, but this recipe makes it so! This focaccia is chewy, fluffy, flavourful and can be made on a single day!

Ingredients

  • 500 g strong flour (W300, 12 g protein)
  • 12 g fresh yeast OR 4-6 g active dry yeast (better 4g during summer or if your kitchen is warm)
  • 1 tsp honey (or malt or sugar)
  • 380 - 400 g lukewarm water
  • 2 tbsp EVOO for the dough
  • 2 tsp of salt
  • additional EVOO (Extra Virgin Olive Oil) for the baking tray and the top
  • some water for the top
  • coarse salt for the top

Steps

  1. Put sugar and yeast in warm water, let sit til slightly frothy
  2. Measure flour and salt
  3. Add water yeast mix
  4. Mix in olive oil
  5. Mix and cover bowl, let rise for an hour
  6. Press onto tray, let rise again for 40 mins
  7. Fold over a few times flatten on oiled baking tray , put finger holes across the dough . Top with kosher salt. Garlic. Rosemary then bake for 30 mins.
